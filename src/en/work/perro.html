Perro.

A hot dog joint with a punk-zine voice. Two things matter: the bun and the sausage.

Visit the site ↗

What I saw

A hot dog joint with two things of its own, the bun and the sausage, that charges more than a corner-store hot dog and less than any other dinner. The trap in this category is the 1950s American diner: chrome, gingham and neon.

The idea

“Yes, it costs more than a corner-store hot dog.”

Objection number one is what’s so special about it, so the bun and the sausage get explained before any price. The voice is a punk zine, the page is a printed collage, and a squirt of mustard marks your progress and works as navigation.
