Dog groomer.

WhatsApp booking and an AI-made 3D illustration for every cut. This is the sample version, without the shop’s details.

Visit the site ↗

What I saw

A neighborhood dog groomer with a 5.0 on Google, no website and no social media: everything ran through WhatsApp. Almost every review names the same person, and the existing photos didn’t show the cuts well.

The idea

“Your dog already knows him.”

One person, the same hands for ten years. The cuts hang from a line with clothespins, like the bandanas the dogs leave with; each card flips to show the cut on a real dog. The booking comes out as a printed ticket and opens WhatsApp with the message ready.

Inside

How I used AI

ToolWhat forWhat I fixed by hand
GeminiTwenty 3D dog illustrations, one per cut, based on the real photos.I cut them out with a script: green background and watermark removed.
Claude CodeThe booking: form, printed ticket and a ready-made WhatsApp message, with price and duration by dog size.After sending, the form is left empty.
Sample scriptBuilds this copy to show to other groomers.It fails if any detail from the original shop is left.
