END 2 END.

Multiplayer bingo for operators. The trade-show schedule is a bingo card.

Visit the site ↗

What I saw

A provider of multiplayer bingo, player accounts and iLottery, with nine licenses and in the market since 2005, and a site that didn’t show the product working. ICE Barcelona is in January 2027: the show where the year’s meetings get booked.

The idea

“End to end, literally.”

With their own player account platform, the name is finally true: from the ball draw to the payout, in one integration. The hero shows a 90-ball room that plays itself, and the trade-show schedule is a bingo card: booking a meeting means marking a square.

Inside

How I used AI

ToolWhat forWhat I fixed by hand
GeminiEight studio images: resin balls, the logo chip and backgrounds.The balls came out without numbers on purpose. I placed the logo’s “2” in code, warped to the measured ellipse of the chip.
Claude CodeThe bingo room that plays itself, eight live screens and the trade-show schedule as a card.The taken slots on the card are samples, and it says so.
DroppedA generated video and the share image.The video was an almost-still card; the image drew the chip hollow.
