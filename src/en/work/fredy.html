Fredy restobar.

Finger sandwiches in Buenos Aires’ north side. A site that doesn’t take the order: it sends people fast to the channel they already use, and builds the office catering order on its own.

Visit the site ↗

What I saw

A sandwich shop with 35 years in the neighborhood and a site that was a WordPress template: Google had indexed sample posts about sushi and cocktails. Orders already came in through WhatsApp and PedidosYa, and office catering was quoted by hand, one message at a time.

The idea

“Most places use two-day-old bread. Ours arrives in the morning and gets sliced on the spot.”

The owner says that in a reel, and it rules the whole site. Everything else gets out of the way: pickup goes to WhatsApp, delivery goes to PedidosYa, and catering is built on an order ticket that adds up pieces, price and how many people it feeds.
