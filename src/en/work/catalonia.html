Catalonia Sur.

Direct bookings with a deposit for an apart-hotel in Bariloche, and a dashboard that shows what it no longer pays Booking.

Visit the site ↗

What I saw

An apart-hotel with a spa above Lake Nahuel Huapi that relied on Booking, and its commission, to fill its nights. Guests loved it and all complained about the same thing: stairs and slopes. The place closed later; the site stayed on as a sample for other hotels.

The idea

“You choose by height.”

The building climbs the hillside, and each type of unit sits at a different height, with a different view. The site turns the complaint around: the stairs are what you’re choosing. Booking means picking a height, then dates on a calendar that won’t let you cross a taken night, then paying the deposit. On the other side, a dashboard adds up the commission that didn’t go to Booking.
