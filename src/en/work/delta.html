Delta Charter.

Yachts, helicopter and private jet from one company. The hero drops from sky to water as you scroll.

Visit the site ↗

What I saw

A yacht, helicopter and private jet company with a 5.0 on Google from 81 reviews, and a 2022 site with one photo of the bay and two buttons. No page said which boats they had, how many people fit, or that they also fly.

The idea

“By air and by water, from Nordelta.”

No other charter company in the area has a jet and a helicopter. The hero says it without words: as you scroll, the video goes from the jet above the clouds to the helicopter over the Delta to the table set on the yacht, and a glass boarding pass changes with each leg. The inquiry goes out on WhatsApp, already written.

Inside

How I used AI

ToolWhat forWhat I fixed by hand
Gemini (video)Three chained shots: jet above the clouds, helicopter over the Delta, the bay at blue hour.Each shot’s last frame is the next one’s first. I cut the jumps and redid the endings where the wing warped.
Python + ffmpegJoins the shots (851 frames) and darkens to night blue at the seam, without spending another generation.One known flaw remains: a 4-frame ghost trail on the helicopter’s turn.
Claude CodeThe scroll that plays the video forward and back, and the inquiry that goes out with the four answers their WhatsApp asks for.The first palette came out generic and I threw it away. It was designed only once there were references.
