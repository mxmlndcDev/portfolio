Evelia.

Their own illustration, animated in layers, changing with the restaurant’s service hours.

Visit the site ↗

What I saw

A restaurant recommended by Michelin in 2025 and 2026, with its own illustration of grandma’s house that the site used as a still background, and a hamburger menu. Reservations sell out month after month.

The idea

“Evelia’s house.”

The restaurant is grandma’s house, and the home page lives on the service’s schedule: sun at lunch, lit windows at night, shutters down on Mondays. When the month’s bookings open, a banner goes up across the street. The menu is read live from where they already edit it, so nobody has to learn anything new.
